# 1. Storefront guide (for customers)

## Creating an account & logging in

You can shop without an account (guest checkout). Creating an account lets you save addresses, see order history, write reviews, and manage your wishlist more easily.

### Sign up with email or mobile

1. Open **Sign in** from the site header (or footer account links).
2. Switch to **Sign up** / **Create your account**.
3. Enter your name, email **or** mobile number, and a password.
4. Complete the security check if it appears.
5. Submit — you will be asked to **verify your account** with a one-time code (OTP) sent to your email.
6. Enter the OTP and confirm. You may see a welcome message after a successful signup.

[SCREENSHOT: login / sign-up screen]

### Sign in

1. Open **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email or mobile number and password.
3. Submit.

You can also use **Continue with Google** to sign in with a Google account that has a verified email.

[SCREENSHOT: Google sign-in button on login page]

### Forgot password

1. On the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email or mobile.
3. If your account was created with mobile only, you may be asked for a recovery email (such as Gmail).
4. Enter the OTP sent by email, then set a new password.

### Password setup after guest checkout

If you checkout as a guest, the store can email you a **password setup** link so you can claim the account and see your orders later. Open the link, choose a password, then sign in.

### Account page

After signing in, open **Account** to:

- Edit your **profile** name
- Change email (verified with OTP to your current email; Google-linked accounts have special rules)
- Add or change **mobile number** (OTP is sent to your profile email — you need an email on file first)
- **Change password** (OTP to email)
- Manage **addresses** and open your **wishlist**

[SCREENSHOT: account page overview]

### Log out

Use **Log out** from the account menu or the Account page.

## Managing cart and checkout

### Cart

1. Open the cart icon for the **Cart View** sidebar, or go to the full **Your Cart** page.
2. Change quantities or remove items.
3. Optionally **Move to wishlist**.
4. When ready, choose **Checkout**.

The cart checks stock live. If something went out of stock, checkout is blocked until you fix the cart.

[SCREENSHOT: cart page]

Signed-in shoppers: your cart can sync with your account so it follows you across devices after login.

### Checkout (guests and signed-in customers)

You do **not** need to be signed in to place an order.

1. Go to **Checkout**.
2. Enter a **shipping address**, or pick a **Saved address** if you are signed in (or choose “Enter a new address”).
   - Indian mobile (10 digits starting 6–9) and 6-digit PIN code are required.
   - A common email provider (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, etc.) is expected when placing the order.
3. Optional: tick **Mark this order as a gift** and add a gift message.
4. Review the order summary and delivery charge.
5. Optional: apply a **coupon** (see next section).
6. Choose payment:
   - **Pay online** — pay securely with Razorpay (cards, UPI, netbanking, and other methods Razorpay shows).
   - **Cash on Delivery** — only available when every item in the cart is allowed for COD (based on brands/categories the store has enabled). If COD is not available, it stays disabled with a reason.
7. Tap **Pay now** (online) or **Place COD order**.

[SCREENSHOT: checkout payment options]

**After a successful order**

- Your cart clears and you land on the order confirmation / tracking page.
- Guests may receive a password-setup email so they can return later.
- COD orders stay pending until the store confirms them.

**Free shipping**

- The store can set a free-shipping threshold (shown on product pages when configured).
- Some brands may be excluded — those items still attract shipping even if the cart total looks high enough.

**Failed online payment**

- A failed attempt can still create an order marked as payment failed.
- Open that order and use **Retry payment** (limited attempts — typically up to 3).

1. At checkout, find the coupon field.
2. Enter the code and tap **Apply coupon**.
3. The discount appears in the order summary if the code is valid (active dates, minimum cart, product/brand/category rules, and usage limits all apply).

**Notes**

- First-time visitors may see a marketing popup that suggests a coupon; the site can remember that code and pre-fill it at checkout.
- If a coupon does not apply, you’ll see an error — check the minimum order value and that the items are eligible.

# 2. Admin panel guide (for store staff)

Access is for store team accounts only (roles such as Super Admin, Manager, Staff, Support). Writing changes generally need a role with edit rights. Some actions (delete orders, manage admin users) are Super Admin only.

## Logging in and setting up 2FA

### Sign in

1. Open the admin login page (staff will have the link from the store owner).
2. Enter your admin email and password.
3. If two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled on your account, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.).
4. You land on the **Dashboard**.

[SCREENSHOT: admin login]

### Enable or disable 2FA

1. In the admin menu, open **Security**.
2. Follow the prompts to enable authenticator-based 2FA (you’ll see a secret / setup details to scan).
3. To disable, confirm with your password and current code.

[SCREENSHOT: admin security / 2FA card]

### Admin users (Super Admin)

Under **Admin Users**, a Super Admin can:

1. Create a staff account (name, email, password, role).
2. Remove admin access.

Roles in plain terms:

- **Super Admin** — full access, including user management and order deletion  
- **Manager** — products, orders, coupons, and day-to-day ops  
- **Staff** — read plus basic edits  
- **Support** — focused on orders and reviews  

There is no “edit password/role” screen after create — remove and recreate if needed, or have the person reset via your internal process.

## Managing products

**Menu:** Products

### List products

- Search the catalog.

- Open a product to edit, or choose **New**.

[SCREENSHOT: admin products table]

### Create or edit a product

1. Open **Products** → **New** (or open an existing product).
2. Fill in:
   - Name (web address/slug is generated from the name)
   - Base price and discounted price
   - Shipping per unit, max order quantity, SKU

   - Category, Sub category, Collection, Brand  
   - Age group  
   - Diecast scale (when the category is the toy cars / vehicles type)
4. Add **colour variants** if needed (set a default colour; attach images per variant).
5. Upload or pick **product images** (reorder, delete; file size limit applies).
6. Set **available quantity** and **low-stock threshold**.
7. Save.

[SCREENSHOT: admin product edit page]

**Tip:** Brands, categories, sub categories, collections, and diecast scales are created/renamed/deleted **from the product form dropdowns** (there are no separate “Brands” or “Categories” menu pages).

### Order detail — day-to-day workflow

1. Review customer, address, gift wrap/message, payment method & status, transaction IDs, totals, and line items.

3. **Update status** as the order progresses, for example:
   - Pending

   - Cancelled  
   - Shipped  
   - Delivered  
   - Return requested / approved / rejected

   - **Push to ShipMozo** to create/book the shipment.
   - **Refresh from ShipMozo** to pull the latest tracking (also refreshes when you open the page / periodically).
6. Or set shipment details manually: shipment status (aligned with customer timeline), carrier, tracking number.
7. Super Admins can **delete** an order (stock is restored). Bulk delete on the list is also Super Admin only.

# 3. Troubleshooting / FAQ

## Customers

### “I didn’t get my OTP / password email”

1. Ask them to check spam/junk and wait a minute.
2. Confirm they used a common email provider (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, etc.).
3. For mobile-only accounts, ensure a recovery email is on file.
4. Have them use **Forgot password** again or **Resend OTP**.
5. Staff can send a **password-reset email** from **Customers**.

### “Google sign-in failed”

Common causes: they cancelled Google’s screen, email not verified on Google, or the email already belongs to a password account. Have them try email/password sign-in, or contact support to identify which method the account uses.

### “Cash on Delivery is greyed out”

COD is only offered when **every** item in the cart matches the store’s COD brand/category allow list.  

**Staff fix:** Marketing → Settings → **Cash on Delivery allow list** — add the relevant brands/categories, or explain to the customer that those items are prepaid-only.

### “Payment failed / money deducted but no confirmation”

1. Ask the customer to open the order link from email (or Orders if signed in).
2. If status is payment failed, use **Retry payment** (limited attempts).
3. Staff: open the order in **Orders**, check payment status and Razorpay transaction IDs; confirm or mark refunded as appropriate; never ask customers to pay twice without verifying the gateway.

### “Cart won’t checkout / item out of stock”

Stock is checked live. Customer should reduce quantity or remove the OOS item.  

**Staff:** update **Inventory** (or the product’s available quantity). Reserved stock from open orders can make “available” look lower than expected — check the inventory columns.

### “Coupon not applying”

Check: code spelling, active dates, minimum cart, and whether the cart’s brands/categories/products are in the coupon’s scope. Also check per-customer and total usage limits under **Coupons**.

### “Where is my tracking?”

Tracking timeline stays on **Order Placed** until staff push to ShipMozo (or enter carrier + AWB). After push, ask the customer to refresh the order page; they can also use the public tracking link when shown.

### “I want to return something”

Direct them to Contact / WhatsApp with topic **Return & refund**, and point them to the returns policy page. Staff then update the order’s return/refund status. Customers can later see listed returns under **Returns**.

### “I didn’t get launch emails”

Confirm they completed the shop notify popup or homepage newsletter. Staff can verify under Marketing → **Latest drop signups** and resend a bulk email if appropriate.

### “Wishlist is empty on my phone”

Wishlist is browser-based. It may not appear on a different device or after clearing site data. Have them re-add favourites or use the same browser they used before.

## Staff

### “Customer says free shipping but shipping was charged”

Check Marketing → Settings:

- Free shipping threshold amount  
- Whether the brand is under **Free shipping exclusions**  
- Whether the cart total (eligible items only) actually met the threshold  

### “ShipMozo push failed”

1. Confirm address, phone, PIN, and product tax/HSN fields.
2. Ensure product weight is set (via CSV if missing).
3. Retry **Push to ShipMozo**; use **Refresh from ShipMozo** after fixing data.
4. As a fallback, enter carrier + tracking manually and set shipment status.

### “Stock looks wrong after a cancel/refund”

Deleting an order (Super Admin) restores stock. Cancelling without delete may need a manual inventory adjustment. Always reconcile **Inventory** after unusual payment outcomes.

### “Review never appeared”

Reviews stay hidden until approved under **Reviews**. Check Rejected/Deleted history with the customer’s product and timing.

### “Abandoned cart / review emails not sending”

Marketing → Settings: confirm the feature is **enabled**, idle/delay hours are sensible, then use **Run reminders now** / **Send now** to test. If nothing happens, contact the technical team to verify email delivery is configured on the server.

### “Can’t see Media upload”

Your role may be read-only. Ask a Super Admin or Manager to grant a write-capable role.

### “I locked myself out of admin (2FA)”

Another Super Admin must help via your internal process (Security settings cannot be bypassed from the storefront). Keep backup codes / a second device enrolled when enabling 2FA.

### “COD orders piling up as Pending”

Confirm payment method is COD, then set status to **Confirmed** when you accept the order, pack, and push to ShipMozo.
